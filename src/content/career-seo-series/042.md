# Fresh Graduate CV Sri Lanka: How to Get Interviews Without Years of Experience

A fresh graduate CV should show how your education and practical work prepare you for an entry-level role. It should not imitate a senior professional's career history.

You cannot guarantee interviews, but you can make relevant evidence easier to assess. Begin with suitable vacancies and the capabilities they actually require from a beginner.

## Identify your strongest proof

List internships, final-year work, relevant assignments, volunteering and leadership activities. For each, record what you personally did and what you produced. A short internship can provide strong evidence if the contribution is explained clearly.

Distinguish group outputs from your own work. “Built the reporting dashboard for a group research project” is more precise than claiming the entire project as an individual achievement.

## Make education informative

Include the degree, institution, completion status and useful specialisation. Add results or selected modules when they support the application. Do not let a long subject list displace practical evidence.

If you are awaiting final results, say so accurately. Do not present an expected classification as an awarded result.

## Replace the generic graduate objective

Introduce your professional direction and relevant strengths. An accounting graduate might mention project work in financial analysis and an internship involving reconciliations. A computing graduate might introduce a specific technical project and the role they want.

The summary should connect to evidence lower in the CV. Avoid describing yourself as an expert because you completed an introductory course.

## Prepare for the application channel

Use a readable document, familiar headings and working links. If the vacancy asks for grades, availability or a separate statement, provide those through the requested format as well.

Tailor the emphasis for each role. A graduate programme and a specialist junior position may evaluate different aspects of the same background.

## Build an improvement cycle

Keep a record of applications and feedback. Update the CV when you complete a useful project or gain practical responsibility. If your target roles consistently require skills you lack, use that information to plan development rather than repeatedly changing the font.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should a graduate include a part-time job unrelated to the degree?

It can be useful if it demonstrates relevant responsibility or transferable skills. Explain the connection and keep the detail proportionate.

### Do employers expect years of experience from every graduate?

Requirements differ. Target genuine entry-level opportunities and read the essential criteria rather than assuming all vacancies have the same expectations.
