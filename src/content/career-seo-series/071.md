# CV for Australia: What Sri Lankan Job Seekers Need to Change Before Applying

For Australian applications, adapt your CV to the employer's instructions and the role. Your Sri Lankan experience remains valuable, but unfamiliar context may need explanation.

Do not assume that changing the country name in your summary creates a suitable application. Review terminology, supporting documents and the evidence required.

## Read the complete application request

Check for a resume, cover letter, selection criteria or other statements. Workforce Australia recommends a simple professional layout and tailoring to the job in its [resume guidance](https://www.workforceaustralia.gov.au/individuals/coaching/job-applications/resumes).

Keep any stated page limit or file requirement. A separate selection-criteria response should not be replaced by extra CV pages unless the employer explicitly permits that approach.

## Explain experience to an unfamiliar reader

Add a brief employer description when the organisation may not be recognised. Describe the function, operating environment and your contribution without assuming knowledge of local titles or abbreviations.

Keep official job titles accurate. If a title is company-specific, add a functional explanation rather than replacing it with a more senior Australian-sounding title.

## Make achievements understandable

Use clear actions, scope and verified outcomes. If a financial figure is relevant and permitted, label its currency so the reader does not assume Australian dollars. Avoid unnecessary currency conversion that could become stale or obscure the original basis.

For projects, explain your contribution and the result rather than listing names that carry no meaning outside your previous organisation.

## Separate location from work eligibility

State your current location accurately and explain relocation plans only as relevant. Willingness to relocate is not the same as permission to work. Answer any eligibility questions truthfully and verify immigration or professional-registration requirements with the relevant authorities.

This article concerns application writing, not an assessment of visa eligibility or recognition of qualifications.

## Check the complete package

Review consistency across the CV, cover letter and form. Explain qualifications using their correct names and status. Do not claim local accreditation or experience you do not have.

Save the vacancy and submitted documents for later preparation. Explore [international CV writing support](/services/packages/cv-writing) or the site's [Australia career information](/australia) when planning how to present your background.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Must I remove my Sri Lankan experience?

No. Present relevant experience clearly and explain context an overseas reader may not know. Local experience should never be invented.

### Does a tailored CV establish work eligibility?

No. Work permission and professional recognition are separate matters. Use the employer's questions and official authorities to verify your actual position.
