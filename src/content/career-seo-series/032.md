# The Perfect CV Structure for Sri Lankan Job Seekers

There is no perfect structure for every Sri Lankan CV. The best order brings relevant evidence forward while keeping your background easy to follow.

Start with the employer's requirements and your career stage. Use the structures below as adaptable starting points, not rigid rules that override application instructions.

## A structure for experienced professionals

Begin with your name and contact details, followed by a concise professional summary, relevant skills, experience, education and certifications. Put recent roles first and keep each employer, title and date range together.

Use a brief scope statement if the role needs context, then selected achievements. Avoid separating all achievements from the roles where they occurred; the reader should be able to understand when and where you delivered them.

## A structure for students and graduates

Education and projects may deserve space before a short work history. Include internships, volunteering or university leadership when they demonstrate relevant skills. Label these experiences accurately rather than presenting everything as paid employment.

For a software internship, a well-explained project may be stronger evidence than an unrelated short job. For customer-service work, that job may be highly relevant. The vacancy determines the emphasis.

## A structure for career changes

Use the opening to explain the new direction and identify transferable strengths. Include relevant training or projects prominently, while retaining a clear employment chronology.

Avoid using a skills-only layout to hide your history. A reader should be able to understand both the transition and the actual experience supporting it.

## Make each section earn its space

Ask what question every section answers. Skills identify capabilities; experience provides evidence; education establishes qualifications. A long objective repeating the summary adds little. A separate list of generic strengths may duplicate both.

Keep contact details practical. Include a professional email, phone number and relevant profile or portfolio links. Consider whether any additional personal information is actually needed for the application.

## Test the order with a reader

Ask someone to find your current role, strongest relevant achievement and key qualification. If these are difficult to locate, adjust the hierarchy before changing the visual design.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Must education always come first?

No. It may lead for students, while relevant professional experience usually deserves greater prominence as your career develops.

### Should I use a different structure for every job?

Usually only the emphasis changes. Keep a stable, readable structure unless the application requires a substantially different document.
