# Internship CV Sri Lanka: How to Make Recruiters Notice You

An internship CV should connect your current learning with the work you are ready to support. Specific projects and clear availability make that connection easier to understand.

Recruiters do not need an exaggerated career history from an intern. They need an accurate picture of your skills, preparation and suitability for the placement.

## Read the placement requirements

Check the discipline, required study stage, duration and application materials. Some placements have academic conditions or scheduling requirements. Confirm those facts before spending time polishing a CV for an unsuitable opening.

Where availability is requested, give realistic dates. Do not promise full-time attendance if lectures or examinations make that impossible.

## Select projects that resemble the work

A finance internship may value spreadsheet analysis and careful checking. A marketing internship may value content planning and campaign reporting. Choose projects that demonstrate relevant tasks rather than listing every assignment.

Explain the problem, your contribution and the output. For example, describe how you cleaned survey data and prepared a summary for a university project, rather than writing only “research skills.”

## Show how you work with others

Internship work often involves learning, asking questions and completing tasks under supervision. Evidence from group projects or volunteering can show reliability and communication.

Describe an actual responsibility, such as coordinating deadlines or maintaining shared project notes. Avoid declaring yourself a natural leader without an example.

## Make the practical details easy to find

Include your qualification, institution, expected completion date and relevant tools. Link to a small, accessible portfolio where useful. Check that the link does not reveal private group files or require unexpected permission.

Use a professional email and a readable filename. The employer should not have to search for your contact details or guess which position you want.

## Submit a complete application

Tailor any requested cover letter to the placement. Explain why its tasks fit your learning and what evidence shows you can contribute. Save the vacancy and the submitted documents for interview preparation.

For support with the CV, explore [student writing services](/services/packages/cv-writing). If the letter is the difficult part, see [cover letter writing](/services/packages/cover-letter-writing).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I list every university module?

No. Select modules when they add relevant evidence and leave space for practical projects and responsibilities.

### Can I apply before completing my degree?

Follow the placement's eligibility requirements. State your current study stage and expected completion accurately rather than implying you have already graduated.
