# How to Write a Cover Letter for a Career Change

A career-change cover letter should explain the connection between your existing experience and the new role. It should make the transition understandable without overstating readiness.

Focus on transferable evidence, relevant preparation and a genuine reason for the change. Avoid turning the letter into a complaint about your current career.

## Define the transition precisely

Identify the function you are leaving and the role you are targeting. A move from teaching to learning and development differs from a move into software engineering; each needs different evidence.

Read the vacancy's essential requirements and recognise real gaps. Persuasive writing cannot replace a mandatory qualification or technical capability.

## Select transferable evidence

Look for activities that connect directly to the new work. Teaching may provide training design and assessment experience. Customer support may provide escalation handling and service analysis.

Describe the original context honestly. Do not rename earlier work so that it appears to have been employment in the new profession.

## Show preparation for the change

Include relevant training, projects or practical exposure, with accurate status and scope. A completed project can demonstrate learning more concretely than a broad statement about passion.

An illustrative paragraph might connect reporting responsibilities with a recent data-analysis project. It should distinguish professional reporting experience from newly developed technical skills.

## Explain motivation constructively

Focus on the work you want to do and the connection to your strengths. Avoid criticising former employers or presenting the new role only as an escape.

Do not invent a lifelong ambition. A thoughtful recent decision supported by practical preparation can be credible on its own.

## Keep the application consistent

The CV should contain the evidence the letter interprets. Check titles, dates and qualification claims across both documents. Make the target direction clear on LinkedIn without erasing your actual history.

Ask a reader whether the transition makes sense and which gaps remain unclear. Explore [cover letter writing support](/services/packages/cover-letter-writing) and [CV review services](/services/packages/cv-review) if you need help building a coherent application.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I mention my lack of direct experience?

Be honest where relevant, but focus on specific transferable evidence and preparation rather than repeatedly apologising.

### Can I change old job titles to fit the new field?

No. Keep official titles accurate and explain relevant duties in plain language. Do not create a misleading employment history.
