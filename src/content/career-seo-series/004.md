# CV Maker vs Professional CV Writer: What Is the Real Difference?

A CV maker helps you arrange information. A professional CV writer helps you decide what that information should communicate to an employer.

Both can be useful for Sri Lankan job seekers. The right choice depends on whether your difficulty is formatting a clear career story or working out that story in the first place.

## What a CV maker can do well

A builder can provide consistent headings, spacing and downloadable files. If you already have strong achievements, accurate dates and a clear target role, a straightforward template may be sufficient. Check the export before committing: can you edit the document later, and does the downloaded version contain readable text?

The limitation is that a template cannot verify your contribution. A box labelled “Experience” will accept a vague duty just as readily as a strong example. Attractive formatting does not make the underlying evidence more relevant.

## What you should expect from a writer

A writer should ask about the roles you want, the work you actually performed and the results you can support. They should help distinguish your contribution from your team's results, explain unfamiliar industry terminology and decide which older information deserves less space.

For example, “handled stock” needs clarification before rewriting. Did you reconcile inventory, investigate discrepancies, receive deliveries or coordinate replenishment? The answer determines the skills and evidence the CV should show.

## Compare the deliverables, not the label

Before buying either option, check:

- Whether the final file is editable and the download has additional charges.
- Whether the service includes content rewriting or only layout changes.
- Whether someone checks the CV against your intended role.
- Whether revisions and turnaround expectations are explained.
- Whether your personal information is handled appropriately.

Neither a builder nor a writer can guarantee that an employer will shortlist you. A useful service explains its process and limitations without selling a universal ATS pass.

## A practical way to choose

Try drafting your summary and three recent achievements without a template. If the information is specific and clearly relevant, start with a simple builder. If you cannot decide what to emphasise, or you are changing careers, consider a review or a writing service.

You can also combine the approaches: develop accurate content with professional feedback, then maintain it in an editable document. Keep a master copy so that future applications do not depend on continued access to a paid platform.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a free CV template unprofessional?

No. A simple free template can present strong content effectively. Assess readability, export quality and your ability to update it.

### Will a writer invent achievements for me?

A responsible writer should not. They can ask questions and improve wording, but every claim must remain accurate and defensible.
