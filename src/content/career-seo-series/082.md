# How to Write a Cover Letter for a Job in Sri Lanka

A useful cover letter explains why you are interested in the role and which evidence makes you relevant. Keep the focus on the employer's opportunity and your actual preparation.

Read the application instructions first. The required format may be a document, email or text field, and each needs an appropriate amount of detail.

## Identify the role and main requirements

Write down the job title and a few priorities from the advertisement. Match each priority to a real example from your background. Choose the strongest examples rather than trying to cover every line of the vacancy.

Research the employer through reliable public information. Use a specific, truthful reason for interest rather than generic praise about being a reputed organisation.

## Open with relevance

Introduce the role and a relevant connection. An illustrative opening could explain that your experience coordinating customer requests and preparing service reports relates closely to the advertised operations position.

Avoid making the first paragraph a long account of your personal needs. The reader needs to understand why the application deserves consideration.

## Develop one or two examples

Explain the situation briefly, your contribution and the relevant result or output. Do not copy the CV word for word; add context that helps connect the evidence to the role.

If you are a graduate, use projects, internships or other genuine responsibilities. Do not apologise repeatedly for limited experience or imply experience you do not have.

## Explain interest and close clearly

Connect your motivation to the work, organisation or opportunity using facts you can support. End with a professional expression of interest in discussing the application and any practical information requested.

Use a named recipient only when you know the correct person. Do not invent a hiring manager's name or rely on an uncertain contact from an old posting.

## Review before sending

Check the employer name, role title, attachments and contact details. Compare dates and claims with the CV. Read aloud to remove awkward phrases and keep the tone natural.

For help selecting evidence or expressing a transition, explore [cover letter writing support](/services/packages/cover-letter-writing). Keep a saved copy of the final application so you can prepare consistently for an interview.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should the letter repeat my whole CV?

No. Select a few relevant examples and explain their connection to the opportunity. The CV carries the fuller career record.

### Can I write the letter in simple English?

Yes. Clear, accurate language is more effective than complicated wording that obscures your meaning or sounds unlike you.
