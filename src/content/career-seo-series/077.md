# CV for Canada: What Sri Lankan Professionals Should Know Before Applying

For Canadian job applications, prepare a focused resume that explains relevant experience clearly. Your Sri Lankan background should be contextualised, not hidden or replaced.

Read the employer's instructions before choosing length or supporting documents. Different professions and application processes can require different information.

## Start with official resume guidance

Canada's Job Bank recommends concise, tailored resumes and advises against unnecessary personal information and photographs. See its [resume-writing guidance](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/resources/write-good-resume?wbdisable=true).

Use that guidance as a starting point, then follow the vacancy's specific requirements. A specialist or academic application may need a different document package.

## Explain unfamiliar experience

Give a short description of employers or projects when the reader may not recognise them. Identify the function, scope and your contribution. Preserve official titles and add a clear functional explanation where useful.

If a result includes money, label the currency and keep the basis accurate. Do not imply Canadian experience simply because you worked with an international client.

## Select evidence for the target role

Prioritise achievements and responsibilities that match the vacancy. Use recognisable terminology where it accurately describes your work. Explain transferable skills through examples rather than claiming experience with tools you have not used.

Keep qualifications accurate. Do not declare Canadian equivalence or professional recognition without verification through the appropriate process.

## Be clear about location and eligibility

State where you are based and answer work-permission questions truthfully. Relocation interest is not the same as permission to work. Verify immigration and regulated-profession requirements with official authorities.

This guide concerns writing and presentation, not an assessment of eligibility or immigration pathways.

## Prepare a consistent application

Check the resume, cover letter and form for matching dates, titles and qualification status. Use the requested file format and test links. Save the submitted version and vacancy for interview preparation.

For help presenting your experience, explore [international CV and resume writing](/services/packages/cv-writing) or [cover letter support](/services/packages/cover-letter-writing). Writing support can improve clarity but cannot guarantee employer interest or recognition of credentials.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I remove all Sri Lankan employer names?

No. Keep the true employers and explain their context when useful. Do not replace local experience with misleading descriptions.

### Does a Canadian-style resume establish work permission?

No. Resume presentation and immigration eligibility are separate. Verify your status and answer employer questions accurately.
