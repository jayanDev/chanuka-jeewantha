# Is Paying for a CV Writing Service Worth It in Sri Lanka?

Paying for CV writing can be worthwhile when you need help explaining your experience, choosing a career direction or preparing a complex application.

It is less useful when you expect the purchase itself to produce an interview. Hiring depends on your suitability, the employer's requirements and factors beyond the document. Start by identifying the problem you want professional help to solve.

## Diagnose the problem before buying

Review several recent applications. Were you qualified for the essential requirements? Did your CV show the requested experience? Were the roles in the right location and at an appropriate level? If the mismatch is eligibility or missing technical experience, rewriting alone will not resolve it.

If your background fits but the CV is disorganised, difficult to read or silent about your achievements, writing support may address a real weakness. A limited review can sometimes identify those problems without requiring a full rewrite.

## Where professional help can add value

Consider an operations professional applying for a first management position. Their CV may describe daily tasks but omit shift coordination, colleague training and escalation decisions. A careful writer can ask for this evidence and present it accurately, without pretending the candidate already held a manager title.

Career changes, long employment histories and international applications can also require choices that a template does not make. The value comes from those decisions and the quality of the finished writing.

## Compare the cost with the scope

Ask what the quote includes before deciding whether it is reasonable:

- An initial discussion or questionnaire about your background.
- Rewriting of your summary and experience, not just grammar correction.
- An editable document and clear delivery arrangements.
- A defined revision process.
- Guidance on adapting the CV for later applications.

Compare the [current packages and prices](/pricing) against those needs. Avoid assuming that a more expensive package is automatically suitable, or that you must purchase a cover letter and LinkedIn service at the same time.

## Set realistic success measures

You should be able to explain your target role more clearly, find your strongest achievements quickly and update the document without breaking its structure. Those are concrete improvements you can inspect immediately.

Over time, record the roles you apply for and the responses you receive. A change in response rate can have several causes, so do not attribute every outcome to the CV. Use feedback to refine both your applications and your target list.

If money is tight, begin with the site's [free career resources](/resources), prepare your own draft and seek focused feedback. Professional support is an option, not an entry requirement for a serious job search.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is CV writing worth paying for as a graduate?

It can help organise projects and internships, but a graduate with a clear draft may only need a review. Choose according to need and budget.

### Should I pay for a guaranteed job result?

Be cautious. A writer can promise specified deliverables, but cannot control an employer's interview or hiring decision.
