# Your Cover Letter Should Never Repeat Your CV — Here Is What It Should Do Instead

A cover letter can refer to CV evidence, but repeating the full career history wastes its main opportunity: explaining why selected experience matters to this role.

Treat the CV as the record and the letter as a focused interpretation. The facts should agree while the amount of context differs.

## Choose a small number of connections

Read the vacancy and identify the priorities your background best supports. Select one or two examples that need a little explanation. Do not summarise every employer in chronological order.

If the CV says you coordinated reporting, the letter can explain the audience, challenge and relevance to the advertised work. It should not simply restate the same bullet in a longer sentence.

## Explain motivation with evidence

Describe why the role's work interests you and why the organisation is a suitable context. Use reliable public information or your genuine experience, not generic praise.

Avoid invented referrals or personal connections. A straightforward reason for interest is more credible than a dramatic story that is not true.

## Clarify a transition where useful

A career change may need an explanation of how earlier experience connects to the new function. A graduate may need to show how a project prepares them for practical work.

Keep the explanation positive and specific. Do not apologise for your background or claim that transferable experience is identical to direct experience.

## Add context without adding contradictions

The letter can expand an example, but should not increase your authority, change dates or introduce unsupported results. Check that your CV contains enough evidence for the main claims the letter makes.

If an example was a team achievement, explain your contribution. Do not convert it into an individual result because a first-person letter makes that convenient.

## Edit for a clear purpose

Label each paragraph privately: introduction, evidence, motivation or close. If two paragraphs do the same job, combine them. If a paragraph does not support the application, remove it.

Read the CV and letter side by side. The reader should gain useful context from the letter without receiving a conflicting story. Explore [cover letter support](/services/packages/cover-letter-writing) if you need help making that distinction.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I mention an achievement already on my CV?

Yes. Use it selectively and add relevant context or explain its connection to the role, rather than repeating it word for word.

### Should the letter include new facts?

It can include relevant context, but major career claims should remain accurate and consistent with the application record.
