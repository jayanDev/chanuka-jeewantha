# Should You Use AI to Write Your Cover Letter?

AI can help organise a cover-letter draft, but you remain responsible for every claim and the final wording. Treat its output as material to review, not a finished application.

The strongest use is to improve structure and clarity using accurate source information. The weakest use is asking it to invent a convincing career story from a job title alone.

## Prepare facts before drafting

Gather the vacancy, your relevant experience and genuine reasons for interest. Remove sensitive details that are unnecessary for the task before using an external tool.

Do not upload confidential employer records, identity documents or other people's personal information simply to obtain more detailed wording. Understand the tool's data-handling arrangements first.

## Give a bounded writing task

Ask for a concise structure using only the facts supplied. Tell the tool to identify missing information rather than invent it. You can also ask it to shorten a paragraph or flag vague claims in your own draft.

Avoid requests such as “make me sound like the perfect candidate.” That framing can encourage unsupported confidence instead of accurate relevance.

## Verify every substantive claim

Check titles, dates, qualifications, tools and numerical results. Remove invented company facts and exaggerated descriptions of your role. Verify any employer-specific information through reliable sources.

If the draft says you led a project, confirm that you actually held that responsibility. If it mentions a percentage improvement, identify the source before keeping it.

## Restore your own voice

Read the letter aloud and replace language you would not use or cannot explain. Keep the tone professional and direct. Avoid turning every sentence into a grand statement of passion or transformation.

Compare the letter with the CV so the facts and level of seniority agree. A polished paragraph is not useful if it creates a contradictory application.

## Follow application rules

If an employer gives instructions about AI assistance, follow them. Do not use a tool to fabricate experience or evade a stated assessment requirement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can AI guarantee a unique or successful letter?

No. Review for generic wording, factual errors and fit. No writing tool can guarantee an employer's response.

### Should I copy an AI draft without editing?

No. Verify every claim, tailor the content and make sure the wording reflects your actual experience and motivation.
