# Professional CV Sri Lanka: How to Position 3–10 Years of Experience Properly

With several years of experience, your CV should show how your contribution has developed. A longer list of duties alone does not communicate professional progression.

Choose the next role you want and explain the experience that prepares you for it. Keep your history accurate while giving more space to recent, relevant work.

## Identify your professional direction

Decide whether you are deepening a specialism, moving into management or changing function. Each direction requires different evidence. An application for a senior technical role should not be written as if people management is the only form of advancement.

Use the summary to introduce that direction through capabilities you already demonstrate, not through a title you hope to hold.

## Show growth within the experience section

Look for increased independence, more complex work, larger scope or responsibility for others. Explain promotions with separate titles and dates. If your title stayed the same, describe how the work changed.

For example, moving from preparing a report to owning its quality checks and presenting findings shows development. You do not need to invent a promotion to make that progression visible.

## Select evidence rather than repeating duties

Give recent roles enough context and selected achievements. Reduce repetitive detail in earlier roles while preserving the chronology. A first job may need only a concise entry if its duties no longer support the target.

Use results you can verify and explain your contribution to team outcomes. Where numbers are unavailable, describe delivered improvements, scope or complexity.

## Update education and skills priorities

Relevant professional work may now deserve more prominence than school details. Keep qualifications accurate and add current certifications where useful. Remove obsolete tools that distract from the role you want.

Skills should reflect work you can discuss at interview. Do not leave an introductory course labelled as expertise simply because it has been on the CV for years.

## Make the next step credible

Read the CV beside a representative vacancy. Identify where the evidence supports its responsibilities and where a genuine gap remains. Use that review to improve both the document and your target selection.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I remove my first job?

Not automatically. Keep an accurate history and useful evidence, but reduce detail when recent roles are more relevant.

### Can progression be shown without promotions?

Yes. Greater scope, independence, technical depth and responsibility can demonstrate development even when the official title did not change.
