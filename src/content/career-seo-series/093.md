# Cover Letter for Overseas Jobs: What Sri Lankan Applicants Should Know

An overseas cover letter should explain relevant experience to an unfamiliar employer and address the specific opportunity. It should not become a generic statement about wanting to leave Sri Lanka.

Focus on the work, your evidence and accurate practical circumstances. Country conventions and application requirements vary, so read the vacancy carefully.

## Explain why the role fits

Select a few responsibilities from the advertisement and connect them to your experience. Provide brief context for local employers or projects the reader may not recognise.

Keep official titles and qualifications accurate. Explain unfamiliar terms without inventing local equivalents or implying professional recognition you have not obtained.

## Make motivation professional and specific

Describe what interests you about the role's work or the organisation. Use reliable public information and your genuine career direction. Avoid generic praise or claims that the employer is your only route to a better life.

Personal motivation may be real, but the letter should help the employer assess professional fit.

## Address location and availability truthfully

State current location or relocation plans when relevant. Answer work-permission and sponsorship questions through the requested process accurately. Do not imply that willingness to relocate equals permission to work.

Immigration and professional-registration questions require current official guidance. A cover letter cannot determine eligibility or guarantee sponsorship.

## Use evidence that travels well

Explain the task, your contribution and the output rather than relying on local prestige or unexplained acronyms. Label monetary figures by currency if they are necessary and permitted to disclose.

If the example was a team achievement, identify your part. Avoid overstating the result to compensate for not having destination-country experience.

## Review the full submission

Check format, length limits and supporting-document requirements. Compare the CV, letter and application answers for consistent facts. Search for another employer or country's name before uploading.

Keep a copy of the submitted version for interview preparation. Explore [overseas cover letter support](/services/packages/cover-letter-writing) and [international CV writing](/services/packages/cv-writing) when you need help communicating your background clearly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I mention relocation in every letter?

Include it when relevant or requested, and keep the statement accurate. Do not let practical details replace the main evidence of role suitability.

### Can a strong letter overcome a mandatory licence requirement?

No. Explain your actual status and verify formal requirements. Writing cannot replace a credential or authorisation the role requires.
