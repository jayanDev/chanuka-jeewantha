# UAE CV Format: What to Include and What to Remove

A UAE CV should contain relevant professional evidence and the practical details needed for the application. Do not include every personal field found in a regional template.

Employer expectations vary by industry and organisation. Begin with the vacancy and keep a clean, adaptable document rather than assuming one format is compulsory.

## Include a clear professional introduction

State your actual career level and relevant strengths. Use experience, skills and qualifications to support the opening. Avoid a summary that targets unrelated professions at once.

Explain unfamiliar employers and internal titles briefly. Keep official titles accurate and clarify the function when needed, without making the role appear more senior.

## Include practical details carefully

Use current contact information and truthful location details. Provide availability or work-status information when relevant or requested, but do not imply authorisation you do not hold.

Treat immigration status as a separate factual matter. A CV format cannot establish eligibility, and this article is not immigration advice.

## Remove unnecessary exposure

Do not place passport scans, identity numbers or confidential employment records inside a generally circulated CV. Verify the employer and purpose before providing sensitive documents through a separate process.

The UAE government's [job-search page](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/searching-for-a-job) includes advice on checking job offers. Use official guidance and the employer's verified channels when assessing requests for additional information.

## Remove weak professional content

Cut generic objectives, repetitive duties and unsupported claims. Replace skill ratings with clear capabilities and explain important achievements through your contribution and verified results.

If a photo or specialist information is requested, follow the actual instruction. Do not assume that every UAE employer has the same requirement simply because a template includes it.

## Check the submission

Use readable headings and consistent dates. Follow the requested file type, inspect the export and test links. Compare the CV with the application form and LinkedIn so the underlying facts agree.

Keep the vacancy and submitted version for later preparation. A [CV review](/services/packages/cv-review) can help assess what to remove, while [international CV writing](/services/packages/cv-writing) can help select stronger evidence for a specific opportunity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Must a UAE CV include a passport number?

Do not assume so. Assess verified application requirements separately and avoid placing sensitive identity information in a widely shared document.

### Should I include my expected salary?

Provide it through the requested application field or discussion when asked. Do not add it automatically if it is unrelated to the CV instructions.
