# How to Write a CV With No Work Experience: A Guide for Sri Lankan Students

Having no paid work experience does not mean having no evidence for a CV. Projects, study, volunteering and practical responsibilities can demonstrate useful capabilities.

The important distinction is how you label that evidence. Present student and volunteer work honestly instead of trying to make it resemble years of employment.

## Choose a realistic target

Read internship, trainee or entry-level vacancies and identify recurring requirements. Look for evidence of basic tools, communication, organisation and subject knowledge that you can actually demonstrate.

Do not apply the same opening to unrelated professions. A CV for a finance internship should highlight different projects from a CV for a social-media role.

## Build the strongest sections first

Include your education with the exact qualification and completion status. Add relevant projects, technical skills and any practical experience. An unpaid society role can be useful if you explain the work rather than merely listing membership.

For a project, identify the problem, your contribution, tools used and output. “University database project” is weaker than an explanation of the records you designed and the queries you wrote.

## Use everyday responsibility as evidence

Helping with a family business, coordinating an event or tutoring can demonstrate real skills. State the context accurately and avoid inventing formal job titles.

An illustrative example is organising registrations for a student event, maintaining the attendee list and coordinating updates with the organising team. This shows activity and responsibility without claiming commercial management experience.

## Keep the document focused

Use a short introduction, relevant education, selected projects, practical activities and skills. Avoid a long paragraph apologising for your lack of experience. Employers recruiting beginners need evidence of readiness to learn and contribute at that level.

Remove generic skill ratings and unsupported claims. If you name Excel or Python, be prepared to explain something you have actually done with it.

## Check before applying

Proofread, test links and confirm availability details requested by the employer. Ask a lecturer, supervisor or trusted reviewer to check whether the evidence matches the role.

Start with the [free career resources](/resources) or explore [student CV writing support](/services/packages/cv-writing) if you need help organising your first application.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I include unpaid work?

Yes. Describe its real context and your contribution. Do not label volunteering or a university project as paid employment.

### What if I have very few activities?

Present the relevant evidence you do have clearly. Build experience through suitable projects or opportunities rather than filling the CV with invented claims.
