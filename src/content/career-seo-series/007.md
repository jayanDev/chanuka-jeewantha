# 10 Signs Your CV Needs a Professional Rewrite

A CV can become outdated even while your career is progressing. These ten signs help you decide whether minor edits are enough or a deeper rewrite would help.

Use them as a document review, not as a diagnosis of every unsuccessful application. A rejection does not prove that your CV caused the outcome.

## Check the direction and opening

1. **Your target role is unclear.** If your opening could introduce an accountant, salesperson or administrator equally well, replace general ambition with a specific professional direction.
2. **Your summary describes an earlier career stage.** A profile written for your first job may now hide supervisory experience or specialist knowledge.
3. **Your strongest evidence appears too late.** Relevant projects, qualifications and achievements should be easy to locate without reading every page.

An opening does not need dramatic language. It needs to give the reader a useful interpretation of the evidence that follows.

## Review the experience section

4. **Every bullet begins with a duty.** “Responsible for reports” says little about the audience, decisions or improvements involved.
5. **Different roles sound identical.** Repeated bullets can conceal promotions, increasing complexity and greater independence.
6. **Results are unsupported or exaggerated.** If you cannot explain where a percentage came from, investigate or replace it with an accurate description of scope.

For example, “improved operations” could become “introduced a weekly handover log so the next shift could track unresolved customer requests.” That is more specific without inventing a numerical result.

## Inspect relevance and usability

7. **Old information dominates.** Detailed school activities may crowd out recent professional achievements.
8. **The file is difficult to edit or read.** Broken page breaks, overlapping text and inconsistent dates need attention before another application.
9. **You send the same emphasis to unrelated roles.** An account-management application and an operations application may use the same facts but need different priorities.
10. **Your CV contradicts LinkedIn or the application form.** Check titles, dates, qualification status and claims about responsibility across all three.

## Decide what level of help you need

Mark each issue as a factual correction, a formatting issue or a positioning problem. Several small formatting corrections may be manageable yourself. If the document no longer tells the right career story, rewriting is more useful than changing colours or fonts.

Before seeking help, gather a recent job advertisement, your current CV and notes about achievements omitted from it. That gives a reviewer something concrete to assess and reduces the risk of receiving generic advice.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should I rewrite my CV?

Review it when your target role, responsibilities or career stage changes. Small updates after meaningful achievements can prevent the need for a rushed rebuild.

### Are few interview calls proof that I need a rewrite?

No. Check role suitability, eligibility and your application approach as well as the CV before deciding what needs to change.
