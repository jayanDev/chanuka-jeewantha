# CV for New Zealand Jobs: A Guide for Sri Lankan Applicants

A New Zealand application should make your relevant experience, qualifications and practical circumstances clear. Explain your Sri Lankan background for a reader who may not know its context.

Begin with the vacancy and use official career guidance where useful. Avoid assuming that every employer or profession requires the same template.

## Read the requirements as a complete package

Check the requested CV, cover letter, application questions and file limits. New Zealand's official career resources include a [CV-writing toolkit](https://api.careers.govt.nz/assets/Uploads/how-to-write-a-CV.pdf) with editing and document-readability checks.

Use the employer's instructions to determine the final scope. A generic template should not override a specific request for evidence or a supporting statement.

## Explain the work and setting

Briefly describe employers or projects that may be unfamiliar. Identify the function, your responsibility and the output or verified result. Keep official titles accurate while clarifying internal terminology.

For example, a service-coordination role can explain the customers, teams and tasks involved. The reader should not need prior knowledge of a Sri Lankan organisation to understand your contribution.

## Present qualifications carefully

Use exact qualification names and completion status. Explain relevant specialisations or training, but do not claim local equivalence or professional recognition without verification.

If the role requires registration or a licence, check the actual requirement through the appropriate authority. This article does not establish eligibility for regulated work.

## Keep practical information truthful

State current location and usable contact details. Answer questions about availability, work permission and relocation accurately. Being interested in moving does not mean you already have permission to work.

Do not include unnecessary identity-document copies in a generally shared CV. Verify legitimate requests before providing sensitive information separately.

## Show relevance through examples

Select achievements that match the role and make your personal contribution clear. If you include a portfolio, check access and permissions. Align dates and claims across the CV, letter and application form.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I remove local qualifications?

No. Present them accurately and explain useful context. Recognition or equivalence should be verified separately rather than assumed.

### Is a New Zealand CV enough to apply for work permission?

No. Career documents and immigration processes are separate. Consult current official requirements for your circumstances.
