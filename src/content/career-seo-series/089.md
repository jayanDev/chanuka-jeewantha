# How to Write a Cover Letter for an Internship

An internship cover letter should connect your learning to the placement's tasks and explain what you can contribute at your current level.

The letter does not need a long employment history. It needs relevant evidence, genuine interest and accurate practical details requested by the employer.

## Understand the placement

Read the discipline, study-stage requirements, duration and responsibilities. Identify the tasks your education or projects have prepared you to support.

If the placement has academic conditions or scheduling expectations, check that you can meet them. Do not promise availability that conflicts with examinations or compulsory study.

## Introduce the connection

State your programme or current stage and identify the relevant preparation. For a finance internship, that might be spreadsheet work and an accounting project. For marketing, it could be content planning and analysis for a university activity.

Use your actual background rather than copying a generic statement about being passionate about the industry.

## Explain one concrete example

Describe the task, your personal contribution and the output. If it was group work, identify your part. Explain how the example relates to the placement without claiming that academic work is identical to professional responsibility.

An illustrative example might describe cleaning survey data and preparing a presentation for a research assignment. This can demonstrate careful analysis and communication when those skills matter to the internship.

## Explain why this placement interests you

Connect your motivation to the work and learning opportunity. Use verified information about the organisation where relevant, and avoid generic flattery.

Balance learning with contribution. The employer should understand both what you hope to develop and what preparation you already bring.

## Check practical details

Include requested availability or placement requirements accurately. Keep the letter concise, use the correct role and employer name, and verify attachments. Align study status and project claims with the CV.

Save the submitted documents so interview preparation uses the same evidence. Explore [internship cover letter support](/services/packages/cover-letter-writing) or [student CV writing](/services/packages/cv-writing) when you need help presenting the application clearly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I mention that the internship is compulsory for my course?

Yes, when relevant or requested. Explain the practical requirement accurately without making it the only reason the employer should consider you.

### Can I reuse the same internship letter?

Reuse a structure, but adapt the organisation, tasks, evidence and motivation to each placement.
