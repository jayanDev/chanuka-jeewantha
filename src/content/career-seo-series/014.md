# 15 ATS CV Mistakes That Could Be Costing You Interviews

ATS CV mistakes include both document problems and misleading content. Review these fifteen issues before assuming that a missing keyword explains every rejection.

The list is a practical editing tool. It does not claim that every employer uses the same software or that each mistake automatically causes rejection.

## File and layout problems

1. **Ignoring the requested file type.** Follow the vacancy or upload instructions first.
2. **Submitting an image instead of readable text.** Check whether you can select the words in the exported file.
3. **Leaving a broken reading order.** Copy the text elsewhere and inspect how columns or floating elements are read.
4. **Using icons without written contact details.** Make your email and phone number unambiguous.
5. **Hiding essential content in decorative elements.** Keep role history and qualifications in ordinary document text.

Always inspect the uploaded version where a preview is available. A good source document does not guarantee a clean export.

## Structure and factual problems

6. **Unclear section headings.** “My journey” is less precise than “Professional Experience.”
7. **Dates detached from roles.** Associate each date range with the correct employer and title.
8. **Inconsistent employment history.** Compare the CV with the application form and LinkedIn.
9. **Unexplained internal job titles.** Add a truthful functional description when a company-specific title would confuse an outsider.
10. **Incorrect qualification status.** Distinguish completed, ongoing and planned study.

A clear structure also benefits the human reader. Fixing factual relationships is more important than chasing a cosmetic score.

## Relevance and credibility problems

11. **Omitting a skill you actually used.** Name relevant tools and responsibilities precisely.
12. **Copying an entire advertisement.** Demonstrate the requirements through your own experience instead.
13. **Adding skills you cannot defend.** A keyword may create an interview question you cannot answer.
14. **Repeating the same words without evidence.** One contextual example is more useful than several unsupported mentions.
15. **Assuming a checker score guarantees shortlisting.** Different tools measure different things; the employer makes the decision.

## Fix the most consequential issues first

Work through the CV in three passes: factual accuracy, relevance to the vacancy, then file presentation. This prevents spending an hour aligning headings while leaving an incorrect date or missing requirement unresolved.

Keep a clean master document and a record of each application version. After substantial changes, ask someone to read the CV without your explanations and describe what they understand about your career.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I fix all fifteen issues even if the portal accepted my file?

Fix any that apply. Successful upload does not establish factual accuracy, relevance or the quality of the reading experience.

### Can these checks guarantee interviews?

No. They improve application quality, while shortlisting still depends on suitability, competition and the employer's process.
