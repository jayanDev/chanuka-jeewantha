# Supply Chain & Logistics CV Writing: Keywords Recruiters Look For

A supply chain CV should identify which parts of the operation you understand and the work you actually performed. Keywords are useful when they describe real process experience.

Procurement, inventory, warehousing, transport and planning are related but distinct. Choose the target function before assembling a skills list.

## Map your place in the process

Describe the flow you supported, such as purchase orders, inbound deliveries, stock control or dispatch coordination. Explain your responsibility and the teams or suppliers involved.

Avoid claiming end-to-end supply chain ownership if your role covered one stage. Specific scope gives a clearer picture of your expertise.

## Use terminology with evidence

Terms such as demand planning, inventory reconciliation, supplier coordination and warehouse reporting should connect to actual work. Name ERP or warehouse systems where relevant, with the tasks you performed.

An illustrative example might describe investigating stock-record discrepancies and coordinating adjustments through the approved process. That is more informative than listing “inventory management” without context.

## Explain measures precisely

Lead time, stock accuracy, delivery performance and cost are different measures. If you claim an improvement, identify the period and definition. Do not compare unlike operations or use a team-wide result without explaining your contribution.

Where numerical outcomes are unavailable, describe an implemented control, planning output or coordination improvement. Keep customer and supplier information confidential.

## Distinguish operational and management responsibility

Supervising a shift, coordinating transport and setting supply strategy involve different authority. Use verbs that match your role. Include team or site scope when it helps interpret the work.

If you are targeting a planning role, prioritise analysis and forecasting-related evidence you genuinely have. For warehouse roles, relevant operational controls and delivery evidence may deserve more space.

## Build a focused application

Compare the CV with the vacancy and remove terms that are unrelated or unsupported. Keep an accurate employment history while selecting the strongest process examples.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I list every logistics term in the advertisement?

No. Include terms that accurately describe your experience or clearly labelled learning, and support important skills with examples.

### Can I include confidential supplier savings?

Only at a level you are authorised to disclose. Use permitted ranges or describe the process contribution when exact figures are private.
