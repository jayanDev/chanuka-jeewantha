# How to Write a Professional CV in Sri Lanka: Step-by-Step Guide

Write a professional CV by choosing a target role, collecting accurate evidence and organising it for the employer who will read it. Formatting comes after those decisions.

This sequence works for a first draft or a substantial update. Keep a complete private record of your background, then create a focused application document from it.

## Step 1: Define the application

Read a representative vacancy. Identify essential qualifications, main responsibilities and relevant tools. Write down which requirements you can support directly and which need a careful explanation of transferable experience.

Do not begin with a template that decides your priorities for you. A student may need prominent projects; an experienced professional may need recent achievements first.

## Step 2: Gather the facts

Collect employment dates, official titles, qualification names and relevant project details. For each role, record what you did, the scope and any result you can verify. Distinguish individual contributions from team achievements.

Use this stage to resolve uncertain dates or numerical claims. It is easier to correct the source information now than after several application versions exist.

## Step 3: Draft the evidence sections

Write recent experience and projects before the summary. Use specific actions and explain their purpose. “Prepared reports” could become “prepared weekly order-status reports for the operations team, highlighting unresolved delivery issues.”

Include education and certifications with accurate completion status. Select skills you can demonstrate rather than filling space with generic personal qualities.

## Step 4: Write the opening

Summarise your professional direction, relevant strengths and supporting context in a few clear lines. The profile should introduce what the document actually proves. Avoid claiming seniority or expertise beyond your background.

## Step 5: Format and verify

Use readable text, consistent dates and familiar headings. Open the exported file, check all pages and test links. Compare the CV with the application form and LinkedIn. Ask someone to identify your strongest relevant evidence without additional explanation.

Save a master and a separate copy of each submission. Update the master after meaningful projects or promotions so the next application is easier. For support, explore [CV writing packages](/services/packages/cv-writing) or begin with a [CV review](/services/packages/cv-review).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I include every job?

Preserve an accurate career history, but adjust the detail according to relevance. Older roles may need only brief entries.

### Can I write a strong CV myself?

Yes. Clear evidence, careful editing and useful feedback matter more than purchasing a particular template or service.
