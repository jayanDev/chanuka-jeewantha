# Executive CV vs Regular CV: What Actually Changes?

An executive CV uses many of the same basic sections as other professional CVs. What changes is the level of context, accountability and impact the evidence must explain.

The distinction is not a more expensive-looking template. It is a clearer account of decisions and responsibility at the level of the role you are targeting.

## The opening describes leadership scope

A general professional profile may introduce a function and technical strengths. An executive profile should also make the leadership environment understandable: business scope, strategic responsibilities and relevant areas of transformation or growth.

Avoid filling the opening with broad claims such as visionary or transformational. Show why those descriptions would be justified through the evidence that follows.

## Experience focuses on consequential contribution

Operational tasks can establish context, but executive entries should explain significant priorities, decisions and outcomes. What did you lead? What authority did you hold? What changed under your responsibility?

Distinguish executive accountability from advisory or project involvement. The reader should not have to guess whether you made a decision, recommended it or implemented one part of it.

## Metrics require context

Business results can be persuasive when the period, baseline and scope are clear. Do not present forecast savings as realised savings or company-wide growth as an individual result without explanation.

Some contributions are better demonstrated through governance changes, delivery milestones or capability development. Financial metrics are not the only valid evidence.

## Supporting experience becomes more selective

Earlier roles establish progression, but do not need equal detail. Education remains accurate and visible while recent leadership work takes priority. Include board, advisory or professional appointments only when genuine and relevant.

Keep confidential information appropriately general. An executive CV is a career document, not permission to publish internal strategy or sensitive client data.

## Consistency extends beyond the CV

Compare the CV with your LinkedIn profile and executive biography. Titles, dates and major claims should agree, even when the documents serve different audiences.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is an executive CV always longer?

No. Length depends on the evidence and application requirements. Selective, clear leadership examples matter more than additional pages.

### Can a manager use an executive-style summary?

Use a summary appropriate to your actual scope and target. Do not imply enterprise authority or board responsibility you have not held.
