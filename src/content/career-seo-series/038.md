# Should You Add a Photo to Your CV in Sri Lanka?

Do not add a CV photo automatically because a template includes one. Consider the employer's instructions, the role and the country where you are applying.

For an ordinary application without a photo requirement, a text-focused CV is a sensible starting point. Your qualifications and evidence should remain the main basis of the document.

## Read the request carefully

An employer may request a photo through a separate application form or specialist portfolio. That does not necessarily mean a large portrait belongs on the CV itself. Follow the specific instructions and verify that the request comes from the genuine employer.

If there is no instruction, consider whether the image adds useful professional information. For most office roles, space is often better used for achievements, skills or projects.

## Distinguish a CV from a professional profile

A LinkedIn profile and a job-application document serve different purposes. Choosing to use a professional profile photograph does not create a requirement to put the same image on every CV.

Likewise, a portfolio may require visual presentation relevant to the profession. Keep any requested visual material in the appropriate format instead of assuming that every career document should look identical.

## Consider the destination market

Application conventions vary. Canada's Job Bank states that a photo is not the norm on Canadian resumes; see its [resume guidance](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/resources/write-good-resume?wbdisable=true). Do not export a familiar local template without reviewing the target employer's expectations.

This is practical document guidance, not a claim that one rule applies to every profession or country. Employer instructions and specialist requirements may differ.

## If a photo is genuinely required

Use an appropriate, current image and keep the document readable. Avoid heavy filters, distracting backgrounds or a photo so large that it displaces essential career evidence. Do not include unrelated personal images.

Check the final file size and the quality of the export. The addition should not turn the entire CV into an image or make the text difficult to select.

## Make a deliberate decision

Keep a clean version without a photo so you can adapt quickly. Review the request for each application rather than maintaining one design for every market. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a photo compulsory for every Sri Lankan CV?

Do not assume a universal requirement. Check the specific employer and application instructions before including one.

### Will removing a photo guarantee better results?

No. It is a formatting and relevance decision. Hiring still depends on your suitability and the employer's process.
