# What Recruiters See After Your CV Passes the ATS

There is no universal “passed the ATS” stage. A recruiter may see your original file, application fields, screening answers or a combination of those materials.

Once a person reviews the application, clear relevance and credible evidence still matter. Software-friendly formatting is only part of preparing a useful CV.

## Make the opening useful

Your opening should identify the professional experience most relevant to the role. Avoid a long statement about wanting a challenging opportunity. The reader needs to understand what you can already offer and which evidence supports it.

A candidate applying for a reporting role could introduce their experience with monthly reporting, data checking and stakeholder communication. The next sections should substantiate those capabilities.

## Show responsibility and contribution separately

The recruiter needs enough context to understand the job and enough evidence to understand your contribution. A brief scope statement followed by selected achievements can do both.

For example, explain that you coordinated customer orders across two internal teams, then describe a specific handover improvement you introduced. Do not attribute a whole department's results to yourself without explaining your role.

## Keep progression visible

Use distinct titles and dates for promotions. Explain increased responsibility through the work itself: larger scope, greater independence, more complex decisions or formal supervision. Repeating identical duties under every role makes progression difficult to assess.

Shorter early-career entries can preserve the chronology without competing with recent relevant work. Do not erase older experience if doing so creates a misleading history.

## Anticipate questions about credibility

An interviewer may ask how you calculated a result, which tools you used or what you personally delivered. Review every major claim before submitting. If a bullet was professionally written, make sure you still recognise and understand the work it describes.

Check consistency with LinkedIn and the application form. Different wording is fine; incompatible titles, dates or qualification claims require correction.

## Make the next step easy

Provide current contact details and functioning links. If a portfolio is relevant, select a few accessible examples with clear explanations. Do not make the reader request permission simply to understand the work you chose to showcase.

Use a [CV review](/services/packages/cv-review) to assess the full reading experience. The [ATS audit tool](/tools/ats-cv-audit) can supplement that review, but a numerical result cannot replace judgment about relevance and credibility.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does an ATS-friendly CV guarantee that a recruiter reads it?

No. Application volume, eligibility, timing and the employer's workflow all affect what happens next.

### Should I write only for the recruiter and ignore software?

Prepare for both. Use a readable file and clear terminology while making the career story useful to a person.
