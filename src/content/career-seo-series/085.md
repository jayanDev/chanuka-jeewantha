# How to Start a Cover Letter Without Saying “I Am Writing to Apply…”

You can start a cover letter by connecting your experience to the role immediately. The opening does not need a dramatic hook to be useful.

“I am writing to apply” is not an automatic mistake. The problem is an opening that spends several sentences identifying the application without explaining relevance.

## Start with a matching capability

Illustrative example: “Your Operations Executive vacancy closely matches my experience coordinating customer orders, maintaining service reports and following up on unresolved delivery issues.”

This approach names the role and gives the reader useful context. Adapt it to real experience; do not copy capabilities that do not describe your background.

## Start with a relevant project

For a graduate: “My final-year project analysing survey data, together with my reporting internship, has prepared me to contribute to the junior analyst role advertised by your team.”

The next paragraph should explain the contribution and tools involved. A project-based opening works only when the project is relevant and accurately described.

## Start with a genuine connection to the work

You might explain that the role's focus on training coordination connects to your experience developing learning materials and organising workshops. Keep the connection specific and factual.

Avoid claiming admiration for a company's culture unless you have a sound basis. Public information can support interest in its work, but does not prove private workplace conditions.

## Avoid attention-seeking openings

Do not promise to double revenue, solve every problem or become the company's best employee. Do not invent a referral. A confident opening should make a credible case rather than create expectations you cannot support.

Similarly, avoid leading with an apology for limited experience. Describe the preparation you do have and target roles appropriate to it.

## Test the opening with the next paragraph

The second paragraph should deliver evidence for the first. If the opening claims leadership, the example should show actual leadership responsibility. If it introduces analytical work, explain the analysis.

Keep the letter concise and verify the employer name and role title. Explore [professional cover letter writing](/services/packages/cover-letter-writing) if you need help choosing the strongest opening for a specific vacancy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is “I am writing to apply” forbidden?

No. It is a conventional phrase. You can use it briefly, then move quickly to relevant evidence and motivation.

### Should the opening include a statistic?

Only if it is accurate, relevant and understandable. A clear connection to the role is more useful than a number added for effect.
