# Does Your CV Need an ATS Score? The Truth About Online ATS Checkers

An ATS checker score can be a prompt for review, but it is not a universal grade that employers share. Always ask what the number actually measures.

A tool may assess text extraction, wording, formatting or similarity to a supplied job advertisement. Those are different questions. Combining them into one percentage can make the result appear more certain than it is.

## Inspect the explanation behind the score

Look for specific findings you can verify. “Your email address was not detected” invites a practical check. “Your CV is weak” does not explain what to change. If the tool recommends a keyword, confirm that it is relevant and truthful before adding it.

Run the same reasoning in reverse: a high score does not establish that your achievements are accurate, that your professional level fits the role or that the employer will interview you.

## Use the job description carefully

Some checkers compare your CV with the text you provide. A result based on a different vacancy may be irrelevant to the application you intend to send. A generic job description may also contain broad requirements that do not reflect the employer's priorities.

Review essential qualifications yourself. A tool's positive feedback should not override a missing mandatory credential or an application question you cannot answer truthfully.

## Do not edit only to increase the number

Imagine a checker recommends “leadership” because the advertisement mentions it. If you trained two new colleagues, describe that work accurately. Adding “senior leader” to your headline would be an unsupported change, even if the score increases.

Keep a copy of the original draft and compare readability after edits. If sentences become crowded with repeated terms, reconsider the changes. Improving a measurement is useful only when the document also improves.

## Consider privacy before uploading

A CV can contain personal contact information, employer details and sensitive achievements. Read the tool's data-handling information and use an appropriately redacted copy when testing an unfamiliar service.

Remove identity numbers, confidential customer information and unnecessary referee contact details. You should understand where your document is going before using a free score as an incentive to upload it.

## Combine automated feedback with human judgment

Use a checker for observations, then review accuracy, relevance and clarity yourself. Ask another person to identify your strongest evidence without explanation. Their confusion can reveal a problem a numerical report misses.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is 80% a universal pass mark?

No. A score belongs to the tool and method producing it. It does not define a shared threshold across employers.

### Should I ignore checkers completely?

No. Use clear, verifiable findings as suggestions. Reject changes that introduce inaccurate claims or make the CV harder to read.
