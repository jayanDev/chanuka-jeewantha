# Executive CV Writing: Why Senior Professionals Need a Different CV Strategy

An executive CV should make leadership scope, decisions and business contribution clear. A long operational history does not automatically demonstrate executive readiness.

The reader needs context for the scale of your responsibility and evidence of how you exercised it. Keep claims proportionate to your actual authority and involvement.

## Establish the operating context

Explain the business environment, function or region you led. Include team, budget or operational scope when accurate and appropriate to disclose. Distinguish direct accountability from participation or advisory responsibility.

“Supported a transformation programme” and “held executive accountability for the programme” describe different roles. Use the one that matches the facts.

## Select consequential decisions

Identify changes you led, priorities you set or difficult trade-offs you managed. Explain the action and result without turning each bullet into a lengthy case study.

An example might describe redesigning an operating model and establishing governance for implementation. Add verified business outcomes where available, while distinguishing the team's delivery from your leadership contribution.

## Make achievements comparable

Revenue growth, cost reduction and delivery improvements need context. State the period, baseline or business scope when needed to avoid misleading comparisons. Do not mix forecast benefits with realised results.

For confidential work, use appropriately general descriptions or authorised ranges. A CV should not disclose sensitive customer or commercial information merely to sound impressive.

## Reduce detail that obscures seniority

Routine duties can establish context briefly, but should not dominate the document. Give more space to leadership, strategic decisions, risk management and organisational outcomes relevant to the target.

Keep earlier roles concise while showing the progression that led to current responsibility. A selective history is different from an incomplete or misleading one.

## Align supporting materials

Your LinkedIn profile, executive biography and CV should agree on titles, dates and major claims. They may differ in length and tone, but should communicate a consistent professional identity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does an executive CV need a different design?

It needs an appropriate emphasis and clear hierarchy. Restrained formatting can work well; strategic content matters more than decorative seniority cues.

### Should every achievement use financial metrics?

No. Governance, quality, capability building and delivery can be relevant. Explain the evidence and business significance accurately.
