# How to Write a Leadership Profile for Your CV

A leadership profile should explain the kind of responsibility you hold and the value your experience supports. It should not be a collection of executive adjectives.

Write it after selecting your strongest leadership evidence. The opening must introduce claims the rest of the CV can substantiate.

## Define your leadership context

Identify the function, environment and level of responsibility. Do you lead operations, a technical team, a business unit or a cross-functional programme? What decisions are actually yours?

Be specific enough to help an outside reader. Internal titles and abbreviations may need a short explanation, but should not be replaced with an inflated title.

## Select a few relevant strengths

Choose capabilities that matter to the target role, such as service delivery, transformation, team development or commercial planning. Avoid listing every management term available.

Each strength should connect to an example. If you claim change leadership, the experience section should explain a change you led and your contribution to its implementation.

## Draft a credible opening

An illustrative profile might read: “Operations manager experienced in coordinating multi-site service teams, improving handover processes and developing frontline supervisors. Brings practical experience in workload planning, service reporting and cross-functional problem resolution.”

This is a structure to adapt, not a claim to copy. Add scope or a verified result only when it reflects your background and is relevant to the vacancy.

## Avoid confusing aspiration with experience

If you are seeking your first management role, describe coordination, mentoring and project responsibility accurately. You can communicate readiness for greater responsibility without claiming that you already held it.

Likewise, managing a department does not automatically establish enterprise-wide strategy or board accountability. Use language that matches the evidence.

## Test the profile against the CV

Underline each substantive claim and locate the supporting example. Remove or qualify unsupported claims. Read the opening aloud and replace language you would not comfortably use in an interview.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I call myself a visionary leader?

Specific leadership context and evidence are usually more useful. Broad labels can feel unsupported unless the document explains what they mean.

### Can technical specialists have leadership profiles?

Yes. Technical direction, mentoring and project decisions can demonstrate leadership. Describe the scope accurately without implying people management where none existed.
