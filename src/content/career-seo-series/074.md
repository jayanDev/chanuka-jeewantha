# UK CV Format vs Sri Lankan CV Format: The Differences That Matter

The useful comparison between UK and Sri Lankan CV formats concerns application expectations, personal information and how clearly experience is explained.

Neither country has one template that every employer follows. Compare your current document with the specific UK vacancy rather than relying on broad stereotypes.

## Reconsider personal-information fields

An older CV may devote space to age, marital status or other details unrelated to professional evidence. Review whether those fields belong in the target application and consult the [National Careers Service CV guidance](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/cv-sections).

Keep your name, usable contact details and relevant professional links clear. A location statement should be accurate; do not use someone else's address to imply residence.

## Make local context understandable

Your previous employer or qualification may be familiar in Sri Lanka but unfamiliar to a UK reader. Add a concise explanation where useful, without replacing official names or claiming unverified equivalence.

The same applies to internal job titles. Preserve the title and explain the function rather than changing it to a role you did not hold.

## Prioritise evidence over biography

Choose achievements and responsibilities that support the vacancy. Reduce unrelated detail and lengthy descriptions of early education when more recent evidence is stronger.

Use consistent role entries and readable bullets. A short explanation of what you delivered is more useful than an exhaustive list of every task performed.

## Check the application package

A UK employer may request a supporting statement or specific answers in addition to a CV. Read those requirements carefully and provide direct evidence in the requested format.

Do not treat a longer CV as a substitute for answering a question. Keep examples consistent across the form, CV and letter while adapting the amount of explanation.

## Preserve one factual source

Maintain a master career record, then create a UK application version with appropriate emphasis. Keep the submitted file and vacancy together so you can revisit the exact claims later.

A [CV review](/services/packages/cv-review) can identify which parts of your existing document need adaptation. [Professional writing support](/services/packages/cv-writing) can help when the career story itself needs restructuring.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to rewrite all my experience for a UK CV?

No. Keep accurate relevant evidence and clarify unfamiliar context. The amount of rewriting depends on the quality of the existing document.

### Should I use different employment dates in different country versions?

No. Dates, titles and qualification status must remain consistent. Tailoring changes presentation and emphasis, not the facts.
