# CV + Cover Letter: How to Make Both Documents Tell One Powerful Career Story

Your CV and cover letter should support the same professional direction while serving different purposes. The CV presents evidence; the letter explains selected connections and motivation.

Consistency does not mean identical wording. It means the reader receives one accurate account of your background and why it is relevant.

## Start with a shared evidence bank

Keep accurate titles, dates, qualifications, projects and achievements in one source record. Note your contribution to team results and any limits on what can be disclosed.

Build both documents from that record. This reduces the risk of a strong-sounding letter introducing a claim that the CV cannot support.

## Give the CV the factual structure

Use a clear summary, relevant skills, experience and qualifications. Select achievements for the target role and keep the chronology understandable.

The CV should make evidence easy to locate. Avoid forcing every explanation into it when a short letter can provide useful context for a transition or motivation.

## Give the letter the interpretation

Choose a few examples and explain why they matter to the employer. Add a genuine reason for interest in the role or organisation. Do not repeat the whole employment history.

For a career change, the letter can explain the connection between earlier work and the new direction. The CV should show the real experience and preparation underlying that explanation.

## Run a consistency check

Compare official titles, dates, qualification status, tools and numerical results. Check that team achievements remain team achievements in both documents. Confirm the same target role and employer appear where intended.

Differences in tone are normal: a letter uses first person more naturally, while a CV is concise. Differences in factual responsibility are not simply style choices.

## Review the submission as a set

Use clear filenames and the requested formats. Open both exports, test links and confirm the correct attachments. Save the package with the vacancy so interview preparation uses the same evidence.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can the letter include an achievement from the CV?

Yes. Use it selectively and add context or explain its relevance rather than repeating the bullet unchanged.

### Should the documents use matching design?

A consistent professional appearance can help, but readable content and factual agreement are more important than identical decorative elements.
