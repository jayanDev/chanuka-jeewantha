# Professional Cover Letter Sri Lanka: The Structure Recruiters Expect

A professional cover letter needs a clear introduction, relevant evidence, a reason for interest and a concise close. There is no single layout every recruiter requires.

Use the employer's instructions first, then organise the letter so each paragraph has a distinct purpose. Avoid turning it into a second CV.

## Opening: identify the opportunity and fit

Name the role and introduce the most relevant connection to your experience. Give the reader a reason to continue without using exaggerated claims.

For example, a graduate can connect a relevant project and internship to the work advertised. An experienced applicant can introduce a matching function or responsibility.

## Evidence: explain selected contributions

Use one or two paragraphs to develop the strongest examples. State what you did and why it matters to this role. Keep the context concise and the ownership accurate.

An illustrative example might explain how you maintained a reporting schedule and followed up on incomplete information. If the role needs coordination and accuracy, make that connection explicit without inventing a numerical improvement.

## Motivation: show a real reason

Explain what interests you about the work or organisation. Use a specific public fact or a clear connection to the role's responsibilities. Avoid unsupported praise about the company being the best in its industry.

Interest should be genuine and relevant. Do not invent a personal connection, referral or long-standing admiration to make the letter sound warmer.

## Close: make the next step clear

Express interest in discussing your application and provide requested practical information. Keep the tone professional without demanding an interview or promising unrealistic results.

Use an appropriate greeting and sign-off. If you do not know the recipient's name, address the hiring team rather than guessing.

## Format for the requested channel

A formal attachment may need contact details and a date; an online text field may not. Follow word or character limits and inspect the final document for spacing and page breaks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does every paragraph need a heading?

Usually not for a short conventional letter. Use clear paragraph purposes and follow any requested statement format.

### Should I include a postal address in an online application?

Follow the employer's requirements and the document format. Do not add unnecessary personal detail merely because an old template includes it.
