# Hospitality CV Writing: How to Build a CV for Hotels and International Hospitality Jobs

A hospitality CV should show the service environment, your responsibilities and evidence of reliable guest or operational support. A generic customer-service description misses important context.

Choose the department and role first. Front office, food and beverage, housekeeping, reservations and hotel management require different examples.

## Describe the operating environment

Explain the type of property or service and the function you worked in. Include scope such as shift responsibility, booking coordination or team supervision when accurate and useful.

Do not assume an overseas reader knows your employer. A short description can establish context without making unsupported claims about the property's status or reputation.

## Show service through specific actions

An illustrative front-office example might describe coordinating late-arrival arrangements and communicating room-status updates with housekeeping. A food-and-beverage example might explain handling service preparation and resolving order issues with the kitchen.

These details show how you worked across the operation. Avoid relying only on “excellent customer service” or “guest satisfaction.”

## Use results with care

If you include guest feedback, service metrics or revenue-related outcomes, ensure they are verified and relevant to your contribution. Do not use a whole property's rating as your personal achievement.

Protect guest information. Do not include names, booking details or private complaint records in a CV or portfolio.

## Make skills and availability clear

List relevant systems, operational training and languages with honest proficiency descriptions. State supervisory responsibility accurately. If the application asks about shifts, availability or relocation, answer through the requested channel.

For overseas roles, distinguish willingness to relocate from current location or work permission. Do not imply local experience or authorisation you do not have.

## Tailor for the property and department

Select examples that match the vacancy's service context. A resort guest-relations role and a reservations role may use the same history but require different emphasis.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I include languages?

Yes, when relevant to the role. Describe your proficiency accurately and be prepared to use the language at the level claimed.

### Is a photo always required for international hospitality jobs?

No universal rule applies. Follow the specific employer's instructions and target-market expectations rather than assuming every hospitality CV needs one.
