# Canadian Resume vs Sri Lankan CV: What Needs to Change?

Adapting a Sri Lankan CV for Canada means reconsidering relevance, personal details and unfamiliar context. It does not mean changing the facts of your career.

Use the employer's instructions as the starting point. A document labelled “Canadian resume” is not automatically suitable for every Canadian vacancy.

## Review information that does not support the role

Older templates may include extensive personal categories and school details. Ask whether each item helps assess the application. Canada's Job Bank provides a useful reference for [resume content and presentation](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/resources/write-good-resume?wbdisable=true).

Keep contact details practical and current. Avoid distributing sensitive identity information or other people's contact details without a clear purpose and appropriate permission.

## Make career evidence concise

Select relevant achievements and responsibilities instead of reproducing an exhaustive history of tasks. Give recent matching experience more space, while preserving an accurate chronology.

If several roles repeat similar duties, show what changed in scope or contribution. Do not force a shorter document by removing the context needed to understand your work.

## Explain local terminology

Keep official qualification and employer names. Add a short explanation where an unfamiliar reader needs context. Do not translate a qualification into a Canadian designation without a verified basis.

Likewise, retain real job titles. A functional description can clarify a company-specific title without falsely increasing seniority.

## Adjust the complete application

Check whether a cover letter, questions or separate documents are required. Select evidence for those materials from the same source record so claims remain consistent.

Keep current location and work-status answers accurate. A resume adaptation cannot resolve immigration or professional-registration requirements; consult official authorities for those matters.

## Verify the final version

Follow file and length instructions, open the exported document and test links. Ask a reader unfamiliar with Sri Lanka to explain the work from your descriptions. Their questions can reveal where context is missing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I have to call the document a resume?

Use the employer's terminology where helpful. Correct content and compliance with the application request matter more than the label alone.

### Should I change my location to a Canadian city?

Only if it truthfully represents your location. State relocation plans separately rather than creating a misleading impression of residence.
