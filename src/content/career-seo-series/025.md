# ATS-Friendly Does Not Mean Boring: How to Build a Professional CV That Still Looks Premium

A professional CV can look polished through careful typography, spacing and editing. It does not need elaborate graphics to communicate quality.

For job seekers in Sri Lanka, the useful design goal is a document that feels organised and makes relevant evidence easy to find. Start with content hierarchy, then use visual choices to support it.

## Build the page around the reader's questions

The opening should identify your professional direction. The next sections should show the experience, skills and qualifications that support it. Keep the most relevant evidence prominent without hiding the chronology.

A strong layout gives each section a clear purpose. An oversized name, long generic summary or decorative sidebar can consume space that would be better used for achievements.

## Use a restrained visual system

Choose one readable body font, consistent headings and predictable spacing. Use bold text to distinguish role titles or employers. A subtle accent colour can help navigation, but the document should remain readable when printed without colour.

Avoid extremely pale text. Secondary information such as dates still needs to be legible. Decorative elements should never be the only way a reader identifies a skill or contact method.

## Let writing create the premium impression

Specific, well-edited experience often looks more professional than a busy design. Compare “hardworking team player” with a clear example of coordinating a handover or resolving a recurring reporting issue.

Keep bullets concise enough to scan while preserving the context and your contribution. Remove repetition before reducing font size. Consistent sentence structure makes the page calmer without making every achievement sound identical.

## Separate the CV from the portfolio

If your work is visual, link to a portfolio with selected projects and short explanations. The CV can describe your role and results, while the portfolio demonstrates the work itself.

Check access permissions and remove confidential client material. A link to a portfolio that requires unexpected sign-in or contains unexplained screenshots can weaken an otherwise polished application.

## Verify function before calling it finished

Open the exported file, check all pages and test links. Copy the text into a plain document and inspect the reading order. Ask someone to find your latest role and strongest relevant achievement quickly.

If the page is beautiful but those tasks are difficult, revise the hierarchy. If it is readable but crowded, simplify the wording and spacing. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use colour on an ATS-friendly CV?

Yes, when it remains readable and does not carry essential information alone. Strong contrast and restrained use are practical defaults.

### Do I need a designer for a premium-looking CV?

Not necessarily. Good writing, consistent styles and careful export checks can produce a professional document using a simple template.
