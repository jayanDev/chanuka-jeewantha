# How to Position a Promotion Properly on Your CV

A promotion should show both the title change and the increased responsibility behind it. Clear dates prevent the reader from misunderstanding how long you held each role.

Do not place your highest title beside the entire employment period if you spent part of that time in a different position. Preserve the progression accurately.

## Group roles under the employer

When several roles belong to one organisation, use the employer as the main heading and give each position its own title and date range. This keeps the relationship clear without repeating the company description.

If a role change involved a different legal employer or subsidiary, describe the relationship accurately rather than merging distinct employment histories for convenience.

## Explain what changed

Identify new decisions, team responsibility, project scope or specialist work. A promotion is more meaningful when the reader sees the difference between the roles.

For example, moving from preparing customer reports to reviewing team reporting and coordinating escalations shows increased responsibility. Do not rely on “promoted due to excellent performance” without evidence or context.

## Put achievements in the correct period

Assign each example to the role where it happened. Avoid repeating the same result under both positions. Give the recent, more relevant role appropriate space and keep the earlier entry concise.

If an achievement spans the transition, explain the sequence briefly. Do not make it appear that a later authority level existed at the start of the project.

## Handle acting or temporary roles honestly

Label acting assignments, secondments and temporary supervision clearly. They can provide valuable evidence without being presented as permanent promotions.

Include dates and actual scope. If you covered a manager's absence, explain the responsibilities you assumed rather than claiming the manager's entire career contribution.

## Check consistency elsewhere

Align the promotion dates and titles with LinkedIn and application forms. Different platforms may display grouped roles differently, but the underlying facts should agree.

Keep records of the transition in your private career notes. A [CV review](/services/packages/cv-review) can help identify unclear chronology, while [professional CV writing](/services/packages/cv-writing) can help explain progression across a longer career.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I combine two similar roles into one entry?

You can simplify the presentation if the titles and periods remain clear. Avoid implying that you held the more senior role throughout.

### Should an acting role appear on the CV?

Yes, when relevant. Label it as acting or temporary and describe the responsibility accurately.
