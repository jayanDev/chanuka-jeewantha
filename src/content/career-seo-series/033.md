# How Long Should Your CV Be? The 1-Page vs 2-Page Debate Explained

A CV should be long enough to present the relevant evidence clearly and short enough to avoid unnecessary repetition. Page count is a constraint, not the purpose.

Follow an employer's explicit limit first. Without one, decide according to your career stage, the role and the amount of evidence the reader needs.

## When one page can work

A student or early-career applicant may be able to present education, a few projects and limited experience on one readable page. That works when nothing important is omitted and the text remains comfortable to read.

Do not add filler to reach a second page. Equally, do not remove the explanation of your strongest project simply because an online rule says all first CVs must be one page.

## When two pages can help

Several relevant roles, technical projects or leadership achievements may need more space. A second page is useful when it adds evidence, not when it repeats the summary or lists every routine responsibility.

Keep the first page strong. The opening and recent relevant experience should establish your fit, while the remaining space supports that impression with qualifications and additional evidence.

## Recognise specialist exceptions

Academic, research and some specialist applications can require detailed records or additional documents. Read their instructions rather than forcing publications, teaching and projects into a commercial CV format.

If an employer requests separate statements or selection-criteria answers, prepare those separately. Do not assume that extra CV pages can replace a required response.

## Edit content before shrinking text

Remove duplicated duties, generic objectives and irrelevant detail. Reduce older roles to their necessary context. Combine overlapping bullets without losing your contribution or the result.

Then inspect spacing and page breaks. A page containing only a few lines may indicate a layout issue, while dense tiny text indicates that you have compressed too aggressively.

## Use a relevance test

For every paragraph, ask whether it helps assess suitability for this vacancy. Keep valuable evidence and cut content that does not. Ask a reader which parts feel repetitive or difficult to understand.

A [professional CV review](/services/packages/cv-review) can help make those choices. The goal should be a clearer application, not achieving a page count for its own sake.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a two-page CV too long for a graduate?

Not automatically. Relevant projects and internships may justify it, but one clear page is preferable to two pages of filler.

### Should senior professionals always use three pages?

No. Seniority does not determine a fixed length. Follow the application instructions and include the evidence needed to communicate scope and impact.
