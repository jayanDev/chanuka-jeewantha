# Why Experienced Professionals Should Stop Using the Same CV They Built 5 Years Ago

An old CV can preserve an outdated picture of your professional value. Adding recent roles at the top does not automatically update the underlying story.

Review the document when your responsibilities or career direction change. The useful question is whether it still introduces the professional you are today.

## Revisit the target role

Your original CV may have been written for an entry-level position or a different function. Check whether the summary, skills and examples still support the roles you now want.

Do not keep a broad objective simply because it has always been there. Replace it with a concise introduction grounded in current relevant experience.

## Look for hidden progression

Over several years, you may have gained responsibility for colleagues, projects, clients or decisions. Those changes often disappear when the CV merely repeats the duties of each position.

Identify the work that became more complex or independent. Explain promotions with accurate dates and titles, and show development even where the official title stayed the same.

## Rebalance the space

School details and early jobs may occupy more space than recent achievements. Reduce older detail thoughtfully while preserving a clear history. Keep an older example when it remains unusually relevant.

Remove obsolete tools and generic skill ratings. Add current capabilities only when you can support them with actual work or clearly labelled learning.

## Review the evidence, not just the design

Check numerical claims, qualification status and portfolio links. A result copied through several versions may no longer be accurate or appropriate to share. Resolve uncertainty before submitting another application.

If the file is difficult to edit, rebuild it with consistent styles and ordinary text. Changing colours alone will not fix an outdated emphasis.

## Create a maintainable master

Keep a private evidence bank and update it after meaningful work. Build application versions from that source rather than continually editing whichever old file you find first.

Ask someone to compare your current responsibilities with the impression created by the CV. If they do not match, consider a [professional rewrite](/services/packages/cv-writing). A [CV review](/services/packages/cv-review) can help establish whether a full rebuild is necessary.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does a CV need a complete rewrite every five years?

No. Five years is a prompt to review, not a fixed expiry date. Rewrite when the structure or positioning no longer fits your career.

### Should I remove everything from the old version?

No. Keep accurate, relevant evidence and useful structure. Update what is stale, repetitive or no longer aligned with the target.
