# Data Analyst CV Writing: How to Showcase SQL, Power BI, Python and Business Impact

A data analyst CV should connect tools to questions, methods and useful outputs. Listing SQL, Power BI and Python does not explain what you can analyse.

Select examples that match the target role's business context and technical expectations. Be clear about whether the work was professional, academic or a personal project.

## Start with the question

What decision or problem did the analysis support? Explain the audience and the information needed. A dashboard is easier to assess when the reader knows what its users were trying to understand.

An illustrative project could compare order delays across regions. The useful story includes the question, data preparation, analysis and how findings were communicated.

## Describe the method accurately

For SQL, explain the querying or transformation work. For Power BI, describe modelling, measures or reporting where relevant. For Python, identify the analysis or preparation task rather than listing every library you imported.

Do not imply production engineering ownership if the work was a notebook exercise. Keep the environment and your responsibility clear.

## Show data-quality judgment

Explain how you handled missing records, duplicates, inconsistent definitions or validation. These decisions can demonstrate analytical care more effectively than a long tool list.

Avoid presenting correlation as proof of causation. If your analysis informed a decision, distinguish that contribution from the final business outcome and other factors involved.

## Use portfolio evidence carefully

Choose projects with accessible explanations and reproducible steps where possible. State the source and limitations of public or synthetic data. Remove credentials and private datasets from repositories.

Screenshots should be accompanied by context: the question, your contribution and the interpretation. A visually attractive dashboard alone does not explain analytical quality.

## Tailor the business emphasis

A finance analyst application may require different examples from a marketing or operations role. Select the most relevant evidence and make important domain terms understandable.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need all three tools for every analyst role?

No. Requirements differ. Read the vacancy and present your real strengths without adding tools you cannot use at the claimed level.

### Can a public-dataset project go on my CV?

Yes. Label it accurately, credit the dataset where appropriate and explain your own analysis rather than implying it was client work.
