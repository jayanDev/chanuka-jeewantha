# Australian CV Format: Why Your Sri Lankan CV May Not Work in Australia

A Sri Lankan CV may need changes for an Australian application because the employer expects different information or supporting documents, not because local experience lacks value.

Treat format as a response to the application. Avoid assuming that every Australian employer requires an identical layout or page count.

## Review the structure

Use clear contact information, a relevant introduction, experience, skills and qualifications. Bring the strongest evidence forward while keeping dates and roles easy to follow.

The official [Workforce Australia resume guide](https://www.workforceaustralia.gov.au/individuals/coaching/job-applications/resumes) supports a simple, readable structure. Your own task is to select the evidence that makes that structure useful for the vacancy.

## Remove template assumptions

Check whether old personal-information fields, decorative graphics or lengthy school records serve a purpose. Do not include information solely because a previous local template contained it.

If a specialist employer requests particular details, follow that instruction. Keep a flexible master document rather than defending one format as correct for every application.

## Translate context without changing facts

Explain unfamiliar employers and internal titles in plain language. State qualification names accurately and avoid declaring equivalence without a sound basis. Describe projects through their objective and your contribution.

For example, a branch-operations role can explain the services coordinated and the responsibility held. The reader should not need local knowledge to understand the scope.

## Check separate application requirements

Look for selection criteria, a pitch or a cover letter. The CV provides career evidence, but another document may need a direct answer to specific questions.

Use consistent facts across those materials. Do not change dates or claims to make each document independently sound stronger.

## Verify the final file

Follow the requested format and page limits, check the export and test links. Make current location and contact arrangements clear. Answer work-permission questions accurately; formatting does not resolve eligibility.

Ask a reader unfamiliar with your career to explain what your experience means. If the context is unclear, revise the description before changing the design. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there one compulsory Australian CV template?

No single template should be assumed for every employer. Follow the actual application instructions and use a clear professional structure.

### Should I rename my qualifications?

Use the official qualification names and helpful explanations. Do not claim an Australian equivalent or registration without verification.
