# CV for Dubai Jobs: How Sri Lankan Candidates Can Compete in the UAE Market

A Dubai-focused CV should show relevant experience, clear professional direction and accurate availability. It should not rely on a generic promise that you can do any job.

Choose a role and employer context, then explain why your background fits. UAE recruitment practices differ across organisations and industries, so follow the actual vacancy.

## Make your expertise specific

Identify the function, tools and operating environment you know. Describe your Sri Lankan experience in terms an unfamiliar reader can understand. Explain the scope and your contribution rather than assuming the employer recognises local company names.

For hospitality, that may involve department and service context. For finance, it may involve reporting or reconciliation work. Do not claim UAE-specific experience you have not gained.

## State practical details accurately

Keep your current location and contact information clear. If the application asks about availability or relocation, answer realistically. Being willing to move is different from being based in Dubai or having permission to work.

Do not infer visa eligibility from a CV guide. Verify immigration and professional-registration matters through the relevant official channels.

## Use an honest application approach

The UAE government's [job-search guidance](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/searching-for-a-job) recommends an honest, updated CV and warns candidates to check the authenticity of job offers. Verify the employer before sending sensitive documents.

Avoid distributing passport scans or personal records with every unsolicited application. Provide additional documents only through a legitimate process where the purpose is clear.

## Adapt to the employer, not a universal template

Check requested file types, supporting letters and role-specific details. Do not assume that every Dubai employer requires a photograph or the same personal-information fields.

Use relevant examples, consistent dates and readable formatting. If a portfolio helps, select accessible work you are authorised to share.

## Keep the application coherent

Align your CV, cover letter and LinkedIn profile. Save the submitted version and record the vacancy so later conversations use the same facts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I write that I have UAE experience if I served UAE clients remotely?

Describe the remote client work accurately. Do not imply local employment or residence if that was not your situation.

### Is one Dubai CV suitable for every industry?

No. Tailor evidence to the role and employer. Finance, hospitality, technology and other functions require different examples.
