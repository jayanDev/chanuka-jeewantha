# How to Write a Manager-Level CV That Shows Leadership, Not Just Responsibilities

A manager-level CV should explain how you directed work, supported people and handled decisions. “Managed a team” is a starting point, not sufficient evidence.

Show the scope of your responsibility and the actions that influenced delivery. Be clear about whether you formally managed people, coordinated colleagues or led a temporary project.

## Establish the team context

Describe the function, team structure and your authority. Include team size or operational scope when accurate and useful. Distinguish direct reports from people you influenced through coordination.

This context helps a reader interpret your achievements. Leading a small specialist team can involve different challenges from supervising a large shift operation; neither should be described vaguely.

## Explain leadership through actions

Look for examples of setting priorities, allocating work, coaching, resolving escalations and improving handovers. Describe what you did and why it was needed.

An illustrative bullet might explain introducing a weekly workload review to identify overdue tasks and agree responsibilities. Add an outcome only if you can support it; implementing a meeting is not automatically proof of improved productivity.

## Show judgment as well as activity

Managers make trade-offs involving time, quality, people and resources. Select an example where you evaluated options and guided a decision. Keep confidential personnel details out of the CV.

Avoid blaming former colleagues or employers. Describe the challenge professionally and focus on your contribution to the response.

## Connect people management to delivery

Training and coaching become more meaningful when the reader understands their purpose. Explain whether you helped colleagues adopt a system, improve a process or take on new responsibilities.

Do not claim sole credit for team performance. State your leadership contribution and the result at the appropriate level.

## Position the next management step

Compare your evidence with the target role's scope. If you are moving into management for the first time, present relevant coordination and mentoring honestly without changing your official title.

A [CV review](/services/packages/cv-review) can help identify missing leadership evidence. Explore [professional CV writing](/services/packages/cv-writing) when your current document still consists mainly of operational duties.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I show leadership without direct reports?

Yes. Project leadership, mentoring and coordination can be relevant. Describe the actual authority and context rather than implying formal line management.

### Should I list team size in every role?

Include it when it helps explain scope and is accurate. Team size alone does not establish the quality of your leadership.
