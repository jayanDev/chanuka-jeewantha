# Resume Writing Service in Sri Lanka: CV or Resume — Which One Do You Need?

The document an employer wants depends more on the application instructions and professional context than on whether the advertisement says CV or resume.

In many ordinary job applications, the two terms describe a concise account of relevant experience. Academic and research applications can require a much more detailed CV. Check the purpose before choosing a template or ordering writing support.

## Read the actual requirement

Look for page limits, required sections, file types and separate questions. An employer may request a resume plus responses to selection criteria. Another may ask for a CV, publication list and research statement. Those are different application packages.

If the advertisement is unclear, use the role and employer as context. For a commercial job, start with relevant experience, skills, qualifications and achievements. For a research appointment, look for instructions covering publications, teaching, grants and other academic evidence.

## Do not turn a career document into a biography

A Sri Lankan CV does not need to include every personal detail or every activity since school. The reader needs enough evidence to assess your fit for the role. Older information deserves space when it still supports that assessment.

Likewise, calling a document a resume does not automatically require squeezing it into one page. Follow an explicit limit; otherwise choose a length that communicates the relevant evidence clearly without repetition.

## Adapt the same facts for different uses

Consider a lecturer applying for both a teaching position and a corporate training role. The academic version may need publications and teaching history. The corporate version may emphasise workshop design, participant needs and evaluation of learning.

The candidate should not alter qualifications or invent commercial experience. They should select and explain genuine evidence for each audience. Keep a complete master record so the shorter documents remain consistent.

## Tell a writing service what you need

Share the vacancy or application instructions, your target market and your current document. Confirm whether the service includes:

- A commercial CV or resume for a defined role family.
- An academic CV with specialist sections, if required.
- Separate application statements or cover letters.
- An editable version for future changes.
- Help prioritising evidence rather than simply reducing page count.

This conversation prevents paying for a visually polished format that does not satisfy the employer's request.

## Check the final submission

Use a professional filename, open the exported document and confirm that all requested materials are attached. Compare your dates and titles with the application form. A strong CV cannot compensate for missing a required statement or uploading the wrong version.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I change the heading from CV to resume for overseas jobs?

Use the employer's terminology when helpful, but prioritise the requested content and format. Renaming an unsuitable document does not adapt it.

### Can I keep one master document?

Yes. Maintain a complete factual record, then create shorter application versions that prioritise evidence for each opportunity.
