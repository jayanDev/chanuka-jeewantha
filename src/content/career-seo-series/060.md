# C-Suite CV Writing: How CEOs and Senior Leaders Should Present Their Career Story

A C-suite CV should explain enterprise responsibility, strategic decisions and the outcomes you can credibly connect to your leadership. Titles alone do not establish that story.

Select evidence for the role and organisation you are targeting. A growth-stage business, a complex operating company and a turnaround situation may value different parts of the same career.

## Establish the business context

Describe the organisation's relevant scale, market and operating complexity without disclosing confidential information. Clarify the scope of your executive accountability and any board or regional responsibilities.

Distinguish formal appointments from advisory work. Board membership, board reporting and occasional attendance at board meetings are not interchangeable claims.

## Organise the story around priorities

Identify the major challenges you were responsible for addressing: growth, operating performance, organisational capability, change or risk. Explain the decisions and implementation approach at a useful level of detail.

Avoid turning every role into a list of abstract strategic terms. A reader should understand what you actually changed and what responsibility you held for delivery.

## Use results with disciplined context

State the period and basis of major metrics where needed. Separate planned benefits from achieved outcomes. Acknowledge team and organisational context rather than implying sole personal causation for every result.

Where sensitive figures cannot be shared, use authorised ranges or specific qualitative evidence. Never publish confidential strategy, client information or internal board material simply to strengthen the CV.

## Show the route to executive responsibility

Keep earlier roles concise but meaningful. They can explain sector depth, functional development and the progression toward broader accountability. Give recent relevant leadership work the greatest emphasis.

Education, certifications and professional appointments should support the career story without overwhelming it. Verify current status before using a credential or membership designation.

## Align the executive materials

Your CV, biography and LinkedIn profile may serve different audiences, but titles, dates and significant claims should agree. Prepare interview examples that explain the context behind major achievements.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should a C-suite CV include routine operational duties?

Use them only where necessary to establish context. Prioritise executive accountability, decisions and relevant organisational outcomes.

### Can confidential achievements still be included?

Often they can be described at an authorised level of detail. Use careful wording and respect contractual or organisational confidentiality requirements.
