# How to Match Your Cover Letter With a Job Description

A matched cover letter turns the employer's requirements into a focused explanation of your relevant evidence. It should not reproduce the advertisement in first person.

Use a simple worksheet before drafting. This helps you select examples and recognise gaps without filling the letter with keywords.

## Extract the main requirements

Separate essential qualifications, core responsibilities and preferred experience. Identify the few priorities that define the role. Do not treat every phrase in a long advertisement as equally important.

Check eligibility honestly. If you lack a mandatory requirement, do not write around it in a way that creates a misleading impression.

## Match each priority to evidence

For every priority, record a real task or project and your contribution. Distinguish direct experience from transferable experience and recent learning.

For example, a requirement for coordination might connect to maintaining a shared schedule and following up on incomplete inputs. A technical requirement needs evidence of the actual tool or a clearly described adjacent capability.

## Choose examples with the strongest connection

Select one or two examples that cover the most important needs. Explain their relevance directly rather than expecting the employer to infer it.

An illustrative sentence could connect your experience checking monthly data and explaining exceptions to the role's reporting responsibilities. Keep the claim proportionate to the work you performed.

## Use terminology naturally

Accurate professional terms help clarity, but repeated keywords do not replace evidence. Do not claim a tool you have not used merely because the job description names it.

If your experience is transferable, state the original context and explain the connection. Avoid presenting two different tools or responsibilities as identical.

## Add motivation and verify consistency

Explain why this work and organisation interest you using a genuine reason. Then check that the CV supports the letter's main claims and that names, dates and qualification status agree.

Read the letter without the advertisement and ask whether its purpose is still clear. Explore [cover letter writing support](/services/packages/cover-letter-writing) if you need help selecting evidence or making the connection concise.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I address every bullet in the job description?

Not necessarily in a conventional cover letter. Prioritise the main requirements, unless the employer asks for a response to every criterion.

### What if one example supports several requirements?

Use it efficiently and explain the relevant connection. Avoid repeating the same story in several paragraphs with only the keywords changed.
