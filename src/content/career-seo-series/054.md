# Your CV Has 10+ Years of Experience — But Is It Showing Career Progression?

Ten years of employment does not explain how your professional contribution developed. Your CV should show the changes in scope, responsibility and capability across that time.

If every role contains the same duties, the reader may struggle to see progression. Review the history for meaningful changes rather than simply adding another job entry.

## Map the turning points

Identify promotions, changes of function, more complex projects and increased responsibility. Note when you became accountable for decisions, quality, customers or colleagues that you previously supported.

Career progression is not always upward movement in titles. Deeper technical expertise, broader industry exposure or a deliberate specialist move can also be important.

## Give each role a distinct purpose

Use a short context statement and selected evidence for each relevant position. Avoid copying the same bullet list under every employer. Explain what was different about the environment or your contribution.

For example, an early role might involve preparing schedules, while a later role involves coordinating several teams and resolving resource conflicts. That difference should be visible in the wording.

## Show promotions clearly

Keep official titles and date ranges. If several roles were with one employer, group them under the organisation while giving each position a clear entry. Do not merge the dates so it appears you held the highest title for the entire period.

Explain meaningful changes in responsibility under the relevant role rather than repeating the employer description.

## Reduce older detail thoughtfully

Recent relevant experience often deserves more space. Older roles can be shorter while preserving the career chronology. Keep an older achievement when it remains particularly relevant to the target.

Do not remove information solely to hide a career gap or a lateral move. A coherent, accurate history is more useful than an artificially smooth narrative.

## Connect the past to the next role

Write the summary after reviewing the progression. It should explain your current professional value, not repeat an objective written at the start of your career.

Ask a reader what changed between your first and most recent roles. If they cannot tell, refine the evidence. [Professional CV writing](/services/packages/cv-writing) or a [CV review](/services/packages/cv-review) can help restructure an accumulated document around the career you now have.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Must every career move look like a promotion?

No. Lateral moves, specialisation and industry changes can be valuable. Explain the actual contribution and context honestly.

### Should I mention an old achievement?

Yes, when it remains relevant and adds distinct evidence. Keep it proportionate so it does not overwhelm recent work.
