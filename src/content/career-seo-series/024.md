# How Applicant Tracking Systems Read Your CV

An application system may extract information from your CV, store the original file and make candidate details available to recruiters. The exact workflow varies by employer.

Understanding those separate functions helps you prepare a clearer application. It also prevents the mistaken idea that every ATS reads every CV in the same way and returns one universal score.

## Think in terms of fields and relationships

Your CV contains connected facts: a role belongs to an employer, dates describe that role and achievements explain work performed there. A clear document makes these relationships visible.

If employer names and dates are scattered across a complicated layout, interpretation can become harder. Use a consistent sequence and familiar headings so the professional history is understandable even without decorative formatting.

## Distinguish the extracted data from the original file

A recruiter may have access to both a document and information entered into the application system. Those views can differ if extraction is incomplete or if you type a different answer into the form.

Check any auto-filled fields before submission. Do not assume that uploading the CV means every field is correct. Equally, do not assume that an extraction error automatically prevents anyone from seeing the original attachment.

## Make terminology understandable

Use recognisable names for tools, qualifications and activities. Explain internal abbreviations when an outside reader would not know them. Keep official job titles intact and add a truthful functional clarification if necessary.

For example, “Customer Service Executive — order support and complaint handling” provides useful context if those activities reflect the role. Replacing the title with “Customer Success Manager” would require a factual basis, not just keyword preference.

## Answer screening questions independently

Application questions may request information that is not obvious in the CV. Read and answer them accurately. Do not rely on a sentence in the attachment when the employer has specifically asked for a separate response.

If a field has a character limit, prioritise the requested evidence rather than pasting a whole experience section. Save a copy of substantive answers when appropriate so you can prepare consistently for later stages.

## Test what you control

Open the final file, copy its text into a plain document and check the order. Inspect links and dates. Then review the portal's preview and populated fields before submitting.

These are practical quality checks, not access to the employer's internal settings. Use the [ATS CV audit tool](/tools/ats-cv-audit) as supplementary feedback and a [professional review](/services/packages/cv-review) for document-specific advice.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I know exactly how an employer's ATS ranks applicants?

Usually not from the outside. Public advice and checker scores do not reveal a private employer's complete configuration or hiring process.

### Should I fill application fields when the same information is in my CV?

Yes, when requested. Keep the information consistent and correct any automatically imported errors before submission.
