# CV for UK Jobs: A Practical Guide for Sri Lankan Professionals

A UK-focused CV should make your relevant experience understandable to the employer and satisfy the specific application requirements. Keep the facts of your Sri Lankan career intact.

Adapt the explanation and emphasis rather than inventing local experience or assuming that one document suits every UK profession.

## Use an appropriate professional structure

The National Careers Service describes a CV through contact details, an introduction, education and work history in its [official guidance](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/cv-sections). Use those building blocks to present evidence relevant to the vacancy.

Follow any employer page limit or template. Academic and specialist applications may request additional information that an ordinary commercial CV does not contain.

## Explain unfamiliar context

Briefly describe employers, projects and internal titles where needed. A local company name alone may not communicate the industry or scale of work to an overseas reader.

Use clear examples of your contribution. If an achievement involves a monetary figure, identify the currency and maintain an accurate basis. Do not add unsupported comparisons with UK business results.

## Review personal information

Keep contact details practical and consider whether additional personal fields are necessary. Do not distribute identity-document copies or referee details as part of a general CV without a legitimate reason and appropriate permission.

The employer may ask separate application questions. Assess those requests in their own context rather than assuming every requested field belongs permanently on the CV.

## Address location and eligibility accurately

State your current location and relevant availability truthfully. Do not substitute a UK address simply to appear local. If the form asks about work permission or sponsorship, answer according to your actual circumstances.

Immigration and professional-registration questions require current official guidance. This article explains career presentation and does not determine eligibility.

## Prepare the full application

Check whether a cover letter, supporting statement or competency response is required. Select examples from the same evidence bank so titles, dates and claims remain consistent.

Save the submitted documents for interview preparation. Explore [CV writing support](/services/packages/cv-writing) and [cover letter services](/services/packages/cover-letter-writing) if you need help explaining relevant experience for a particular UK opportunity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I hide that I am based in Sri Lanka?

No. Keep location and eligibility information accurate. Present relevant experience clearly rather than creating a misleading impression of local residence.

### Is a CV enough for every UK application?

No. Some employers request forms or supporting statements. Read and complete the full application requirements.
