# How Much Does Professional CV Writing Cost in Sri Lanka?

Professional CV writing prices depend on the work included, your career history and the documents you need. A price without a scope is difficult to compare.

There is no single fee that reliably represents every Sri Lankan provider. Rather than publishing an unsupported market average, this guide explains how to assess a quote and find the current prices for Chanuka Jeewantha's services.

## Start with the current package page

Use the [pricing page](/pricing) for current fees, and open the relevant package for its inclusions. Prices and offers can change, so confirm the displayed total before placing an order. An old social media post or an article should not override the current package terms.

Check whether your quote covers one target role, several role variants, a review of an existing CV or a complete rewrite. These can involve different amounts of work even when the finished documents have similar lengths.

## Understand what can affect the quote

A student's main task may be organising education and projects. An experienced professional may need several roles rewritten and evidence clarified. A senior leader may need sensitive achievements expressed without disclosing confidential business information.

The number of pages is therefore an incomplete measure of effort. A concise two-page leadership CV can require substantial research and editing. Conversely, moving existing wording into a new template is a different service from developing the content.

## Ask these questions before comparing prices

- Is career targeting included, or am I supplying finished wording?
- Which file formats will I receive, and can I edit them?
- How many revision rounds are included, and within what period?
- When does the delivery period begin: payment or receipt of complete information?
- Are a cover letter, LinkedIn profile or extra role variant separate purchases?
- Is any urgent-delivery charge included in the total?

Get the answers in writing. This helps both you and the provider understand what successful delivery means.

## Avoid a misleading comparison

Imagine one quote covers layout only and another covers an interview, rewriting and revisions. Comparing the headline prices as if they buy the same work hides the important difference. Equally, a premium label is not proof of a better result.

Ask for anonymised examples of writing and an explanation of the process. Evaluate whether the service can communicate your industry experience accurately. Do not pay extra merely for an unexplained ATS percentage or a promise of guaranteed shortlisting.

## Choose a sensible scope

If your existing CV is mostly strong, a [professional CV review](/services/packages/cv-review) may identify targeted improvements. If your career direction or content needs restructuring, [CV writing](/services/packages/cv-writing) may be the more suitable scope. Discuss the problem before choosing a package.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where can I see the exact current price?

Check the website's pricing and package pages, then verify the order total and inclusions before payment.

### Does a higher price guarantee interviews?

No. Price describes a purchase, not an employer's decision. Assess the actual deliverables, writing quality and fit for your needs.
