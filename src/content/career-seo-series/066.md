# Engineering CV Writing: How to Present Technical Projects and Achievements

An engineering CV should explain the systems or projects you worked on, your technical responsibility and how the work was checked or delivered.

The discipline matters. Civil, mechanical, electrical and other engineering roles require different evidence, so avoid presenting a generic list of engineering skills.

## Establish project context

Describe the asset, system or technical problem at an appropriate level. State whether the work involved design, installation, testing, maintenance or project coordination.

Clarify your role and authority. Preparing drawings, reviewing them and approving a design are different responsibilities. Do not imply sign-off authority merely because you participated in the project.

## Explain your technical contribution

Use specific activities and relevant tools. An illustrative example might describe updating equipment drawings, checking dimensions against site information and resolving discrepancies with the project team.

For maintenance work, explain the equipment and diagnostic or preventive tasks involved. For design work, describe the requirement and deliverable without exposing proprietary details.

## Show validation and delivery

Testing, inspection, documentation and handover can demonstrate professional rigour. Explain the checks you performed or supported. If you claim an improvement in reliability or efficiency, identify the measurement basis.

Avoid presenting a simulation result as a verified field outcome. Label prototypes, academic work and operational installations accurately.

## Handle standards and credentials carefully

Include standards, licences or registrations when relevant and genuinely part of your work or qualifications. Verify current status and use exact designations. Do not list a standard solely because it appears in the advertisement.

If a target role requires professional registration you do not hold, do not conceal the gap. State your actual status and follow the employer's requirements.

## Select projects for the next role

Prioritise work that demonstrates the required technical depth, operating environment or project responsibility. Older projects may need less detail when recent evidence is stronger.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I include project value?

Include it only when accurate, relevant and permitted to disclose. Explain your scope so the figure does not imply responsibility for the entire budget.

### Can academic engineering projects be included?

Yes, especially early in your career. Label them as academic and distinguish analysis or simulation from real-world deployment.
